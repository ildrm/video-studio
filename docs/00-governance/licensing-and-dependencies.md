# Licensing and dependencies

Status: research required • Owner: legal and open-source compliance. The workspace contains a GPL v2 license; its applicability to future code, documentation and distribution must be assessed by counsel. Do not assume this grants rights to third-party media, fonts, models or dependencies. Generate an SBOM and record license, version, source, notices, build flags and approval for every dependency and font.
