# Media verification and quality control

Status: proposed design; not implemented or tested. Owner: media engineering lead. Dependencies: [canonical model](../03-frontend/canonical-editor-state-and-schema.md), [feature catalog](../01-product/feature-catalog.md), [release gates](../09-delivery/phase-gates-and-definition-of-done.md).

A successful process exit is insufficient. Verify existence and nonzero size; probe container, streams, codec, duration tolerance, dimensions, rational FPS, audio layout/sample rate and color metadata; decode the full output under limits. Measure configured loudness/true peak after encoding. Scan for black/frozen frames and unexpected silence/dropout as warnings with intervals and thresholds, since intentional holds and silence occur. Verify sidecar timing or burn-in mode and check dependency and snapshot identity. Store an integrity-protected report linked to the immutable artifact manifest with worker/compiler version, timestamps, thresholds, findings and artifact hash.

Hard structural failures block publication; inconclusive or content-sensitive checks must say `unknown` or `review_required`, never pass by default. Generate contact sheets and review crop, safe areas, caption legibility and color; automated CV may aid but cannot prove semantic quality. Compare preview/final golden frames under explicit tolerances; audio tests assess alignment and clipping. Maintain fixtures for VFR, rotated/HDR, odd sizes, no audio, mixed rates and corrupted files, and record baseline and drift on upgrades. Do not claim any checks have run. [FFprobe](https://ffmpeg.org/ffprobe.html) supplies machine-readable inspection; full decode is mandatory for publication; human review applies to content-sensitive warnings and golden-fixture release checks, rather than blocking every ordinary user render.
