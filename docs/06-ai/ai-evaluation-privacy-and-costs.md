# AI evaluation, privacy and costs

Status: proposed design; not implemented or tested. Owner: AI engineering lead. Dependencies: [canonical model](../03-frontend/canonical-editor-state-and-schema.md), [feature catalog](../01-product/feature-catalog.md), [release gates](../09-delivery/phase-gates-and-definition-of-done.md).

Maintain a consented, versioned evaluation set with representative languages, accents, audio quality, motion, lighting and content; separate train/development/test and avoid logging raw content by default. Measure ASR WER/CER and timestamp error, diarization error, translation adequacy, TTS pronunciation/naturalness, highlight acceptance, tracking retention/jitter and plan validity/authorization violations. Record sample size, stratified results, human-review procedure, confidence intervals and unresolved bias. Compare provider and model upgrades against a pinned baseline and rollback on regressions. Proposed targets require product and safety owners; no metric is claimed measured here.

Before dispatch enforce purpose, role, tenant, region, consent, retention and budget; transmit minimal data via scoped encrypted access. Contracts must state provider retention, subprocessors, training use, breach notification and deletion feasibility. Maintain asset/result lineage and purge derived data and provider copies where supported; disclose exceptions. Meter provider units and normalized media duration, inference time, storage and retry charges by tenant and feature; estimate before submission, reserve quota atomically, reconcile actual cost and alert on anomalies. Cap expensive retries and concurrency. Monitor latency, quality drift, refusal, failure and cost without exposing transcripts or signed URLs in logs. Provide opt-out, human review and a manual workflow when AI is unavailable.
