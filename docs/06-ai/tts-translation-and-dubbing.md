# TTS, translation and dubbing

Status: proposed design; not implemented or tested. Owner: AI engineering lead. Dependencies: [canonical model](../03-frontend/canonical-editor-state-and-schema.md), [feature catalog](../01-product/feature-catalog.md), [release gates](../09-delivery/phase-gates-and-definition-of-done.md).

Translation consumes approved transcript segments and produces editable language-specific text with alignment to source cues; retain original, target language, model/version and confidence only if provided. Human review checks meaning, names, timing, cultural context and profanity. TTS consumes approved text, authorized voice, locale and delivery parameters; returns an immutable audio asset, duration and provider provenance. Do not imply translated text precisely matches original timing. Dubbing composes target speech with source and music using explicit ducking/mix controls, and flags overlap or overlong phrases for review.

Voice cloning or identity imitation requires verified rights and explicit informed consent under product policy; deny when proof is unavailable and support revocation. No model training or vendor reuse without separate authorization. Estimate characters, duration and provider cost before execution; enforce quotas and region/retention eligibility. Retries must be idempotent or reconciled to avoid duplicate charges. Measure translation adequacy, pronunciation, timing, speaker consistency and user corrections on consented language-specific fixtures; a metric alone cannot guarantee meaning or safety. Store output as a derived asset with deletion and retention lineage.
