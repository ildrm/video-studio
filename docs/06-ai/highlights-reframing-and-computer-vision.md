# Highlights, reframing and computer vision

Status: proposed design; not implemented or tested. Owner: AI engineering lead. Dependencies: [canonical model](../03-frontend/canonical-editor-state-and-schema.md), [feature catalog](../01-product/feature-catalog.md), [release gates](../09-delivery/phase-gates-and-definition-of-done.md).

Highlight ranking consumes authorized media, transcript and optional scene/audio measurements and returns candidate half-open source intervals, score, model provenance and explanation of evidence; users select or reject every candidate. Loudness or duration are proxies, not evidence of semantic quality. Tracking returns bounded boxes/keyframes with time and optional confidence; convert them into editable crop paths. Segmentation and separation produce versioned derived assets and masks, with clear preview of errors.

Request minimum necessary media at suitable proxy quality; inspect provider retention, region and permissions. Low confidence, occlusion, multiple subjects, scene cuts or missing signal require review and an editable fallback such as center crop. No automated face identity or privacy-redaction guarantee. Bound processing length, resolution and cost, report partial output and allow cancellation. Evaluate candidate precision and user acceptance, subject retention, temporal jitter, crop safety, mask edge quality and performance on diverse lighting, movement and skin tones; use consented labeled fixtures and human review. Track false positives/negatives and retain model and preprocessing versions. Launch only after measured thresholds are approved.
