# Launch checklist and rollout

**Status:** proposed; checklist is not evidence of completion. **Owner:** release manager; product, engineering, SRE, security, privacy and support sign off.

Before launch, record build and immutable image digests, supported browsers and device limitations, contract/schema versions, migration compatibility, CI results, acceptance and accessibility evidence, open severity-ranked defects, legal and codec review, provider consent/retention, tenant isolation and penetration test findings. Confirm tested backups and recovery, quota and abuse policies, alert routing, support contact and incident staffing, status communication, billing reconciliation and data deletion process. Verify actual workloads against capacity/cost thresholds and error budgets; do not infer performance from target values.

Deploy a small opted-in cohort with risky capabilities default off, observe predefined error, latency, queue age, render QC, support and unit-cost thresholds for an agreed window, then expand by decision record. Pause rollout on confirmed data loss, isolation failure, sustained SLO burn, severe accessibility blocker or a material billing error. Revert compatible images or disable features, retain accepted project revisions and audit state, and run the relevant incident procedure. Review cohort feedback and remaining risks before each expansion. Publish accurate capability limitations, provider dependencies, pricing/quota policies and support materials. Post-launch review tracks incidents, adoption, errors and cost without collecting raw media or transcripts in analytics.
