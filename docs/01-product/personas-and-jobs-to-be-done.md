# Personas and jobs to be done

**Status:** Proposed research hypotheses. **Owner:** UX researcher. **Scope:** Persona needs and mode fit; validate with interviews. **Related:** [journeys](user-journeys-and-workflows.md), [catalog](feature-catalog.md).

| Persona | Job, pain and success | Essential capabilities and mode |
|---|---|---|
| Beginner creator | Turn raw clips into a polished post without learning track jargon; confusing tools and failed exports hurt confidence. Success: first downloadable video unaided. | Guided steps, presets, progress, recovery; Quick. |
| Long-form creator | Build a consistent episode from hours of footage; repetitive trimming costs time. Success: reviewed edit with reusable branding. | Pro timeline, captions, markers, exports. |
| Short-form creator | Publish accessible portrait clips rapidly; reframing and caption timing vary. Success: multiple reviewed formats. | Quick, caption editor, versioned presets, later smart reframe. |
| Freelance editor | Deliver precise client revisions; edit loss and ambiguous feedback cause rework. Success: reproducible revision and export. | Pro, versions, timecoded review. |
| Podcast producer | Align long recordings and readable transcripts; drift and noise accumulate. Success: synchronized export and editable transcript. | Audio tracks, sync, captions; Pro. |
| Educator | Produce clear lessons with accessible captions; unfamiliar controls slow teaching. Success: accurate captions and usable delivery. | Quick, caption correction, thumbnails. |
| Marketing manager | Deliver branded variants on schedule; inconsistent logos and permissions create risk. Success: approved exports per campaign. | Brand kit, shared review, batch; Quick plus Pro. |
| Small-business owner | Make an affordable promotion from phone footage; limited time and variable connectivity. Success: completed export with transparent quota. | Guided workflow, resumable uploads; Quick. |
| Agency editor | Manage client projects and assets separately; accidental cross-client access is costly. Success: controlled workspaces and versions. | Tenant isolation, media library, Pro. |
| Creative team manager | Coordinate approvals and workload; status scattered across tools. Success: known revision and approval. | Workspace roles, review queue, usage; later phase. |
| Reviewer/client | Give precise feedback without changing the edit; email timecodes are ambiguous. Success: comment attached to revision/time. | Narrow share link, comments; review surface. |
| Organization administrator | Govern members, usage and credentials; unclear authority creates exposure. Success: least-privilege audit and offboarding. | Membership, audit, quota, policy controls; admin. |

Validate tasks with representative users across keyboard, assistive technology, poor connectivity, RTL and mobile. Never treat the mode recommendation as a restriction. All personas can switch mode without data loss.
